# Controlled prompts from CSFM scene themes (50 × 6)

This collection was used for the single-seed exploratory experiment on September 6, 2026. It is not a standardized benchmark.

There are 300 distinct prompts: 50 each for object, color, shape, texture, count, and spatial relation. Prompts contain 20–37 whitespace-delimited words. Target spans use half-open character intervals. On RunPod, STAR's paired slow and fast CLIP tokenizers agreed for every prompt, all target boundaries were valid, and the longest sequence contained 43 tokens without truncation.

[Review table](review.md) · [Canonical JSON](prompts.json) · [Construction script](build.py)

## Files

- `prompts.json`: the sole canonical experiment input, including targets, subtypes, scene families, and source identifiers. Filter by `semantic` for a category subset.
- `build.py`: deterministically exports the hand-specified combinations to `prompts.json` and `review.md`. It does not load a model.
- `review.md`: readable prompt table, with the intervention target in bold.
- `source_pool.json`: the original caption sample retained for provenance; the construction script selects 10 scene themes from this pool.

Previously exported per-category JSON files were exact copies of subsets of `prompts.json` and have been removed. Rebuilding the data no longer creates these redundant files.

## Sources and construction

Validation captions from [CSFM-ImageNet1K-Caption](https://huggingface.co/datasets/junwann/CSFM-ImageNet1K-Caption) supply scene material. Each experiment record contains the source ID/path and adaptation notes. The source captions were generated by Qwen3-VL-8B Instruct; original images were not inspected.

The experiment uses controlled combinations based on 10 scene themes: a grassy garden, a cafe table, a library, a garden path, a tiled floor, an office desk, a worktable, a kitchen, a desert, and a courtyard. Objects, attributes, counts, and relations may be deliberately replaced. Adapted prompts are not ground-truth annotations of the original images or independent random samples of original captions.

- **Object:** 50 subjects.
- **Color:** 10 colors, 5 prompts each, including common and uncommon object–color combinations.
- **Shape:** 5 geometric outlines, 10 prompts each; targets describe outer object outlines.
- **Texture:** 10 each for striped, checkered, polka-dotted, rough, and smooth. Pattern and surface subtypes are recorded separately.
- **Count:** 10 each for 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6 objects. Background bookshelves were removed from book-counting prompts to avoid extra books.
- **Spatial relation:** 10 each for left/right; 5 each for above/below, front/behind, and inside/outside. Left/right refer to the viewer's perspective; depth and containment can involve occlusion ambiguity.

## Interpretation

Text and token checks preceded generation. No samples were removed or replaced based on generated-image success. Shared scenes, subjects, and wording create dependence, and this run uses only seed 42. Figure shading is an interquartile range, not a confidence interval. Backgrounds, target lengths, and object configurations are not fully matched across categories; category rankings are not pure semantic causal effects.

Full global conditioning and contextual text embeddings remain intact. The intervention blocks target-token attention access only at selected scales. Neither LPIPS nor full-prompt CLIPScore directly measures target-semantic correctness.
